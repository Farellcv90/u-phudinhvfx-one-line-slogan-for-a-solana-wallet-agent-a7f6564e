Entry for the [One-Line Slogan for a Solana Wallet Agent](https://make.fun/bounty/u-phudinhvfx-one-line-slogan-for-a-solana-wallet-agent-a7f6564e) competition on make.fun — one slogan of at most 20 words for a Solana wallet agent, judged pairwise by an AI copy chief on creativity first and clarity second. The winning slogan becomes the wallet agent's public tagline.

## The slogan

> Your Solana wallet hired a concierge: say the word — it signs, swaps, and stakes while your keys stay put.

The pitch: a wallet agent as a concierge — you just say the word, and it handles the signing, swapping, and staking for you, while your keys never leave your pocket. Fresh image, plain words, and an honest promise: it does the work, you keep the keys.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This validates the slogan (single line, 1–140 UTF-8 characters, not only whitespace, at most 20 words) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
